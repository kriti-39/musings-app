# Deva's Classes — Guide for Guruji

**App:** https://app.musingswithdeva.com
**Last updated:** 10 August 2026

---

## 1. Getting in

1. Open **app.musingswithdeva.com** in Chrome (or tap the app icon if installed).
2. Enter your **User ID** and **password** → **Sign In**.
3. You stay signed in — you only log in once per device.

**Install it like an app:** Chrome menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen**. It then opens full-screen from your home screen, like WhatsApp. *(On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. This is required for notifications to work.)*

**Turn on notifications (do this once per device):**
Sidebar → **Settings** → **Notifications** → **Turn on notifications** → Allow.
Below that, pick your **Class reminder** — how long before each class you want a buzz (15 min / 30 min / 1 hr, or Custom up to 24 hours).

---

## 2. The five screens

| Screen | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today at a glance + requests needing your approval |
| **Students** | All students, admins, deactivating people |
| **Classes** | Full list — upcoming, completed, cancelled |
| **Schedule** | The calendar — book classes, block your time |
| **Fees** | Confirm payments, mark cash payments |

On the phone, tap the **☰** menu (top-left) to switch screens. The **🔔 bell** (top-right) shows your notifications.

---

## 3. Dashboard — start here each day

Four boxes at the top: **Total Students**, **Today's Classes**, **Pending Requests**, **This Month**. Tap any to jump there.

**"Needs Confirmation"** appears when something needs you:
- A student booked a slot that clashes with another class
- A student asked to reschedule

Each row shows the student's name and time, with **Confirm** ✓ or **Reject** ✗. Below that, **Today's Classes** lists your day.

---

## 4. Schedule — the calendar

Switch between **Month / Week / Day** at the top. **Today** jumps back to today.

### Book a class yourself
1. Tap **+ Schedule Class** (or tap an empty slot on the calendar, then tap it again)
2. Choose the **student**, **date**, **time**, **duration** (30m / 45m / 1h / 1.5h / 2h)
3. Optionally add **lesson notes** (what you'll cover)
4. For regular weekly students, tick **Make this a recurring class** → choose *every week* or *every 2 weeks* → optionally an end date (blank = 8 classes)
5. **Schedule Class**

The app warns you — but doesn't stop you — if:
- ⚠️ *This time already has a class* (fine for a group lesson)
- 🚫 *This time is blocked* (you marked yourself unavailable — schedule only if it's a deliberate exception)

### Block your time off (concerts, travel, personal)

Sidebar → **Schedule** → **Availability**

1. **From** date — and a **To** date if it's several days
2. Tick **Block full day(s)**, or leave it unticked and set **start & end times** (e.g. 9:00 to 17:00)
3. Optionally a **reason** — private, students never see it
4. **Block this time**

Students cannot book any blocked time.

**If classes are already booked in that period**, you'll see a warning listing every student, date and time:
- **Keep classes** → nothing happens, go back
- **Block & cancel** → the time is blocked, those classes are cancelled, and each student is told: *"Your class has been cancelled — Guruji is unavailable. Please reschedule after consulting with Guruji."*

### If your plans change (concert cancelled)

In **Availability**, tap the 🗑 next to the blocked period. If it had cancelled any classes, you'll be asked:

- **Unblock & restore classes** → the time is free again *and* those classes come back, with students told *"Guruji is available again — your class is back on."*
- **Unblock only** → frees the time but leaves those classes cancelled
- **Keep block** → changed your mind, nothing happens

*(Only future classes can be restored.)*

## 5. Classes — during and after a lesson

**Classes** has three tabs: **Upcoming**, **Completed**, **Cancelled / Rejected**. Tap any class to open it.

Inside a class you can:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Confirm / Reject** | For pending requests only |
| **Mark as Done** | Marks the class completed |
| **Add / Edit Notes** | What was covered — the student sees this |
| **Add Recording** | *(completed classes only)* — see below |
| **Reschedule** | Pick a new date/time; the student is notified |
| **Cancel Class** | Cancels it; the student is notified |
| **Delete permanently** | Removes it completely — cannot be undone |

### Posting a class recording

1. **Classes** → **Completed** tab → tap the class
2. **Add Recording**
3. Paste the link (YouTube / Google Drive / Zoom)
4. Optionally give it a **title** — e.g. *"Raag Yaman — meend practice"*
5. Tips or corrections for the student go in **Lesson Notes**
6. **Save Recording**

The student gets a notification and finds it in their **Recordings** menu, along with the class date and time. Posting a recording is entirely optional — skip it whenever you like.

---

## 6. Students

Three tabs: **Students** (active), **Deactivated**, **Admins**. Search by name, User ID or country; sort by name, country or date joined. Tap any student to see their full history — classes, payments, recurring schedules.

- **Deactivate** — they can no longer log in, and upcoming classes are cancelled. Their history is kept and they can be reactivated any time.
- **Deactivated tab** → **Reactivate** to bring them back, or **Delete** to remove them and all their data permanently *(cannot be undone)*.
- **Admins tab** → **+ Add Admin** — enter name, User ID and password, then share those with your assistant.

> **Note:** adding a *student* is done from an **admin** account, not the teacher account. Ask an admin to add new students.

---

## 7. Fees

Pick the **month** at the top, then filter by status.

- A student has submitted a payment → **Confirm** ✓ (or **Decline** if it's wrong). They're notified either way.
- Paid in cash or by hand → **Mark Paid** — recorded as confirmed immediately.
- If they uploaded a screenshot, tap to view the receipt.

Students never see fee *amounts* or who owes what — only their own payments and whether they're pending or confirmed.

---

## 8. What the app does on its own

- **Past classes complete themselves.** Once a class's finish time passes, it moves to Completed automatically — no need to mark every class done. If one didn't actually happen, open it and delete it.
- **Free slots auto-confirm.** When a student books an empty slot, it's confirmed instantly. Only *clashing* bookings come to you for approval.
- **Everyone stays in sync.** You and your admins share one calendar — anything they schedule or block shows on yours, and vice versa.
- **Times are personal.** Every student sees class times in their own country's time; you see yours.

## 9. Notifications you'll receive

| When | You get |
|---|---|
| A student books a class | *"Ankoo booked a class — Mon, 11 Aug, 06:00 PM."* |
| A booking clashes with another | *"…this overlaps another class, please confirm."* |
| A student asks to reschedule | *"Ankoo asked to reschedule to Tue, 12 Aug, 07:00 PM."* |
| A student cancels | *"Ankoo cancelled their class — Mon, 11 Aug, 06:00 PM."* |
| A student submits a payment | *"Ankoo submitted a payment of ₹2000 for review."* |
| Before each class | *"Your class starts at 06:00 PM — in about 15 minutes."* |

Tapping a notification opens the right screen straight away. They arrive on your phone even when the app is closed — as long as you turned them on in Settings.

## 10. If something looks wrong

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Blank or stuck screen | Close and reopen the app |
| Notifications not arriving | Settings → Notifications → is it "Turn off"? If it says "Turn on", tap it. On iPhone, open the app from the Home Screen icon, not Safari |
| "Notifications are blocked" | Allow notifications for the app in your phone's settings, then try again |
| A class is missing | Check the **Completed** tab — it may have finished and moved there |
| Student says they can't log in | Check they're not in the **Deactivated** tab; passwords are case-sensitive |
| Wrong time showing | Settings → Studio Timezone — make sure it's your city |

---

## 11. Things that cannot be undone

Be careful with these — there's no way back:

- **Delete permanently** on a class
- **Delete** a deactivated student *(removes all their classes and payment history)*
- **Cancel Class** — a cancelled class can't be un-cancelled *(except classes cancelled by a block, which can be restored by removing that block)*

Everything else is safe to explore.
